# Ovia Inc — Studio & Lab

We are Ovia Inc, a studio and lab working in context engineering, AI agent systems, and virtual production — based in the Laurentians and Montréal, working in English, French, and Spanish.

Our practice: durable authority in AI operations. We design the systems around models — the context, memory, tooling, and review loops that decide whether an agent deployment works in production or only in a demo — and we build media with the same engineering discipline.

## What lives here

This organization holds our own projects beside a curated working collection of forks — the open-source shelf the studio evaluates and builds with. Every fork carries an Ovia section at the top of its README: what the project is, why it sits in the collection, and full credit to its upstream.

**The shelves:**

- **Agents & harnesses** — agent runtimes, orchestration, and skills: LangGraph, agency-agents, agents-cli, Antigravity, ECC, superpowers
- **Voice** — realtime voice-agent frameworks: LiveKit Agents, Pipecat
- **Memory & context** — persistent agent memory and shared context layers: Mem0, Letta, Memorilla, Trail Code
- **Models & infrastructure** — local runtimes and model tooling: Ollama, airllm, MiniCPM-V
- **Production** — virtual production and code-first media: cinecode, OpenMontage
- **Security & data** — security tooling, business intelligence, and data workflows: our own data-science, machine-learning-engineering, and artificial-intelligence repositories
